# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project welcomes people who participate in good faith. Be respectful, keep technical discussion focused, and give others room to ask questions or disagree.

Acceptable participation includes:

- discussing ideas and evidence without attacking people;
- giving actionable review feedback;
- respecting privacy and removing sensitive data from reports;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them;
- helping keep issues and pull requests relevant to the project.

Unacceptable participation includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, publishing private communications, or sustained disruption.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions about the project, and other spaces where someone represents the project or its community.

## Reporting and enforcement

For a public moderation request, open an issue and include only information safe to publish. For a confidential report, use [private reporting](https://github.com/Yivas/pi-subagent-models/security/advisories/new) and prefix the title with `[Conduct]`.

Do not publish personal details or sensitive evidence in an issue. The maintainer may edit or remove content, lock a conversation, reject a contribution, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Reports will be reviewed in context; this project does not promise a fixed response time.

Anyone involved in a report should avoid conflicts of interest and protect information shared in confidence.
