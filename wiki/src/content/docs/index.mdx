## What it covers

The override applies to the `subagent` tool, slash-command launches, prompt-template delegation, and `pi-subagents` RPC spawn requests.

:::note
Scheduled runs keep the selection active when they are scheduled. Changing the selector later does not rewrite queued runs.
:::
